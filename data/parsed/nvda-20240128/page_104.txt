Note 13 - Commitments and Contingencies
Purchase Obligations
Our purchase obligations reflect our commitments to purchase components used to manufacture our products, including long-term
supply and capacity agreements, certain software and technology licenses, other goods and services and long-lived assets.
As of January 28, 2024, we had outstanding inventory purchase and long-term supply and capacity obligations totaling $16.1 billion. We
enter into agreements with contract manufacturers that allow them to procure inventory based upon criteria as defined by us, and in
certain instances, these agreements allow us the option to cancel, reschedule, and adjust our requirements based on our business
needs prior to firm orders being placed, but these changes may result in the payment of costs incurred through the date of cancellation.
Other non-inventory purchase obligations were $4.6 billion, which includes $3.5 billion of multi-year cloud service agreements, primarily
to support our research and development efforts.
